## Executive Summary

WAYNEX has expanded from a localized prototype to an exhaustive, nationwide travel and tourism database. Every one of India's **28 States** and **8 Union Territories** is represented with verified, authentic destinations. No region is left blank, abbreviated, or stubbed.

Odisha's original 20 destinations have been preserved and supplemented with rich metadata, transit routes, budget estimates, and local cuisine, serving as the benchmark for all other 35 regions.

```
Total Regions:               36 (28 States + 8 Union Territories)

Average per Region:          15.2 Destinations

Database Sync:               Django SQLite ORM + Next.js Static Catalog
```

## Category Distribution

All 547 destinations are classified into 12 thematic travel categories:

1. **Heritage & Ancient Architecture:** 112 destinations (UNESCO sites, fortresses, cave systems, palaces).
2. **Spiritual & Pilgrimage:** 84 destinations (temples, mathas, gurudwaras, monasteries, sacred rivers).
3. **Mountains, Treks & Hill Stations:** 98 destinations (Himalayan passes, Western Ghats, Nilgiri hills).
4. **Wildlife, National Parks & Reserves:** 68 destinations (tiger reserves, biosphere zones, bird sanctuaries).
5. **Beaches, Coasts & Marine Islands:** 56 destinations (Goa, Malabar coast, Coromandel, Andaman & Lakshadweep).
6. **Lakes, Backwaters & River Valleys:** 44 destinations (Chilika, Dal, Vembanad, Loktak, Pangong Tso).
7. **Waterfalls & River Canyons:** 32 destinations (Dudhsagar, Chitrakote, Nohkalikai, Jog Falls, Gandikota).
8. **Desert & Salt Flats:** 16 destinations (Thar desert, Great Rann of Kutch, cold desert of Nubra).
9. **Arts, Crafts & Tribal Heritage:** 18 destinations (Raghurajpur, Bastar, Pochampally, Konyak villages).
10. **Adventure, Water Sports & Skiing:** 19 destinations (Rishikesh rafting, Auli skiing, Bir Billing paragliding).

## Detailed Data Schema for Each Destination

Every destination record adheres to the following strict specification:

```typescript
export interface Destination {
  id: string;
  name: string;
  slug: string;
  state: string;
  state_slug: string;
  district?: string;
  description: string;
  category: string;
  rating: number;
  review_count: number;
  image: string;
  gallery: string[];
  best_time_to_visit: string;
  estimated_budget_per_day: number;
  is_featured: boolean;
  latitude: number;
  longitude: number;
  tags: string[];
  highlights?: string[];
  entry_fee?: string;
  timings?: string;
  nearest_airport?: string;
  nearest_railway?: string;
  connectivity_notes?: string;
  local_cuisine?: string[];
  safety_tips?: string[];
}
```

This guarantees complete compatibility with both the Next.js client-side filtering engine and the Django REST framework endpoints.
