## 1. Primary Authority Sources

WAYNEX travel intelligence draws upon authoritative, verified repositories:

### A. Geography & Administrative Boundaries
1. **Survey of India (SOI):** Official national boundaries, latitudes, longitudes, and territorial jurisdictions.
2. **Census of India:** District counts, state capitals, official languages, and geographical land area metrics ($\text{km}^2$).
3. **DataMeet India Maps:** GeoJSON boundary contours utilized for 3D subcontinental mapping.

### B. Climate & Seasonal Travel Advisory
1. **India Meteorological Department (IMD):** Climatological normals (1981-2010 / 1991-2020), annual monsoon rainfall patterns, and temperature distributions.
2. **Ministry of Earth Sciences, Government of India:** Regional climate classification across coastal, Western Ghats, Gangetic plains, and Himalayan alpine zones.

### C. History, Heritage & Monuments
1. **Archaeological Survey of India (ASI):** Centrally protected monuments, ancient epigraphic records (Ashokan Rock Edicts, Kharavela's Hathigumpha Inscription, Chola copper plates).
2. **UNESCO World Heritage Centre:** Inscribed Cultural and Natural Heritage Sites (Konark Sun Temple, Mahabodhi Temple, Hampi Monuments, Sundarbans, Western Ghats).
3. **National Museum, New Delhi & State Museums:** Archival records, dynastic timelines, and regional resistance movement chronicles.
